3

Bypass the algo

Every week

Your best odds come from the hiring manager or someone on the team. Personalize the note below.

Subject: Payments PM role Hi [Name], I applied for the Product Manager role on Payments last week, and wanted to make sure my application landed on your radar. Quick background: • 5 years driving checkout and pricing strategy at [Company]. • Reduced checkout drop-off by 18% across 3 core optimization tests. • Deeply familiar with [Target Company]'s API and merchant onboarding flow. I've attached my resume here. If my profile looks like a fit for what you're building, I'd love to be routed into your interview process. Best, [Your Name]

Bonus: if you have specific feedback or an idea for their product, work it in. Frame it as a question, not a critique.

Watch: Your network is your net-worth
4

Generate proof of work

1-2 weeks

Anyone can vibecode a prototype. Deliver a project all the way through.

What counts
  • Something a stranger can open and use, not a screen recording.
  • Evals: 20 or more real cases, the metric you scored them against, and the score you got.
  • One change you made because the evals told you to, with the before and after numbers.
  • A short writeup of what you would fix next, and what you would need to know first.
Pick one
  • Merchant support triage. Pull 30 real questions off the Stripe community forum, build something that routes them by type, then score it against your own labels and report where it fails.
  • Checkout step rebuild. Rebuild one step of a checkout flow in React, put it in front of 5 people, and report what broke and what you changed because of it.
Watch: How to Prove Your Seniority in Tech (It's Not Your Code)
5

Prepare for the interview
  1. Product design"How would you improve Stripe Checkout for first-time merchants?" What they're testing: whether you narrow before you build. Pick one merchant type and one moment they drop off. A feature list reads as avoidance.
  2. Analytical"Payment success rate dropped 3% last week, how do you find the cause?" What they're testing: whether you segment before you guess. Split by country, card network, and merchant size out loud, then name a suspect.
  3. Behavioral"Tell me about a time you shipped with incomplete data. What did you decide?" What they're testing: whether you own a call and its cost. Give the tradeoff you accepted, not the part where it worked out.

Reading these is not practice. Open voice mode in ChatGPT or Claude and answer out loud, using this prompt:

You're interviewing me for a Product Manager role on Payments at Stripe. Ask one question at a time and wait for my full spoken answer. Then tell me the single thing a strong candidate would have said that I didn't. Start with product design.
